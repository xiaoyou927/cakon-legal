Privacy Policy

Cakon is a daily video memory app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information You Create

Videos, notes, and composed films you create are stored on your device. Cakon does not upload your personal media to Cakon servers as part of the core timeline experience.

Permissions

Camera and microphone access are used only when you record a moment. Photo library access is used to choose videos or save finished films. App Tracking Transparency and notification prompts may appear once for promotional measurement readiness; Cakon does not use targeted advertising identifiers or send push notifications in the current product flow.

Voice Feedback and Speech Processing

Data category: microphone audio and the transcribed text of optional Voice Feedback notes.

Recipient: Apple operates as a third party speech recognition provider for optional Voice Feedback transcription on iOS.

Purpose: convert spoken feedback into editable text notes that remain stored locally in Cakon.

Consent: before sending audio for transcription, Voice Feedback shows an Allow confirmation. If you decline, no audio is shared and you can type a note instead.

Purchases

Memory Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Purchase receipts are validated through StoreKit on device. Transaction identifiers may be stored locally to keep credit grants idempotent.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the App Store support channel listed for Cakon.
